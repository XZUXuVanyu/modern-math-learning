## Assistance and follow-up revision

Before submission, the conversation clarified that associativity belongs to the binary operation and changes parentheses without changing element order.

After inspecting the PDF, the tutor identified that the submitted two-element comparison proves commutativity rather than associativity. The focused hint was to compare the two parenthesizations of three arbitrary elements $S(t_1),S(t_2),S(t_3)$.

The learner then supplied this revision in conversation:

$$
S(t_1)*(S(t_2)*S(t_3))
=
(S(t_1)*S(t_2))*S(t_3)
=
S(t_1+t_2+t_3).
$$
