# Lesson rhythm and review

One lesson every two days. Existing schedule: around 19:00 Asia/Shanghai, starting 2026-09-09; M001 delivered 2026-09-07.

The dates organize delivery, not mastery. Preserve each lesson ID and its actual status. A delayed answer creates no assumed advancement or demand for multiple catch-up assignments.

1. Lesson day: brief recap, one new concept/skill, worked example, exactly one homework.
2. Intervening day: learner attempt, feedback, and revision as available.
3. Next lesson: select the next textbook concept or a bounded prerequisite from actual feedback.
4. Weekly checkpoint: summarize attempted lessons, observed obstacles, assistance, and one next action; no separate homework is added.

After three submitted lessons, assess the provisional 20–30 minute study and 20–40 minute homework targets. Adjust abstraction, scaffolding, or task length from actual evidence. Revisit demonstrated concepts after two or three subsequent sessions, using a brief recap or the next lesson's single homework.

The source project's alternating software-structure/physics weeks do not apply here. This course follows textbook dependencies.
